Pregnancy Massage

Comfortable, well-supported massage for a body that's doing a lot of work, in your own home.

What it can help with

  • Lower back, hip, and pelvic discomfort
  • Neck and shoulder tension
  • Tired, achy legs and feet
  • Trouble getting comfortable or relaxing

What to expect

Most of the session is done lying on your side, supported with pillows, so there's no pressure on your belly and you can stay comfortable throughout. We'll adjust your position whenever you need, and bathroom breaks are always fine.

Pressure is adapted for pregnancy. Some areas, like the legs, get gentler work because of the higher risk of blood clots during pregnancy.

Safety first

Your intake form asks about your pregnancy so I can plan a safe session. If your pregnancy is considered high-risk, or you have conditions like preeclampsia, placenta issues, or bleeding, I'll ask that your prenatal care provider OK massage before we book.

Please contact your provider instead of having a massage if you have vaginal bleeding, sudden swelling of your face or hands, severe headache, fever, or pain or redness in one calf.

After birth

Postpartum massage can help with the strain of feeding, carrying, and recovering. If you're breastfeeding, chestfeeding, or pumping, see breast, chest & lactation massage.
