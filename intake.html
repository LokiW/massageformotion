1. Consent to collect consumer health data

Required by Washington's My Health My Data Act. Full details are in my Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

What I collect

Your name and contact details, your address (for home visits), your health history, pregnancy or lactation information if relevant, and your treatment preferences, including which body areas you consent to have massaged.

Why

Only to decide whether massage is safe for you, plan your treatment, and keep a record of your care. I never use it for marketing or advertising.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Your answers are saved in a private Google account that only I can access. Google stores the data for me but may not use it for its own purposes. I do not sell your health data or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

Your rights

You can see what I hold about you, ask me to delete it, or withdraw this consent at any time by emailing loki.for.motion@gmail.com. Withdrawing consent won't affect your ability to book. We'll just use paper forms.
