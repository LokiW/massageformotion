Abdominal & Diaphragm Massage

Your belly and breathing muscles hold a lot of tension that most massage skips.

The diaphragm is your main breathing muscle. It sits under your ribs and works closely with the abdominal muscles, low back, and hip flexors. When these get tight or guarded, often after illness, stress, injury, or surgery, breathing can feel shallow and digestion can feel sluggish. Gentle, focused work on the abdomen, ribs, and diaphragm helps these areas soften and move more freely.

It's a good fit if you have

  • Shortness of breath that lingers after long COVID or the flu, well after you're no longer sick
  • Shallow breathing, or a tight, "can't get a full breath" feeling through the ribs
  • Constipation or bloating
  • Core tension from training, like after heavy lifting, aerial work, or lots of crunches
  • Low back or hip pain linked to tight hip flexors and abdominals

What to expect

You'll lie on your back, usually with your knees supported. I work slowly and adjust to what feels right for you, and I'll often have you breathe into the area I'm working on. Abdominal work can be combined with a full therapeutic session or be the main focus.

The abdomen is only worked on with your consent, and you choose whether it's over the sheet or undraped on the intake forms. You can change your mind at any time.

See a healthcare provider first if breathing difficulty is new, severe, or comes with chest pain, or if you have severe or sudden abdominal pain, blood in your stool, or unexplained weight loss. Please tell me on your intake form about a hernia, recent abdominal surgery, pregnancy, an abdominal aortic aneurysm, or digestive conditions.
