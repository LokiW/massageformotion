Breast, Chest & Lactation Massage

Respectful, consent-based work for an area most massage skips.

Chest tissue holds tension like any other part of the body, and it's closely tied to neck, shoulder, and upper back pain. This service is for anyone, of any gender, who would like it included in their session, including people who are breastfeeding, chestfeeding, or pumping.

Lactation support

Gentle massage may help with:

  • Engorgement and general discomfort
  • Clogged ducts
  • Chest, shoulder, and upper back strain from feeding and holding a baby

This works best alongside your lactation consultant (IBCLC) or provider, not in place of them.

Signs of infection need a provider first. If you have a fever, flu-like symptoms, or a hot, red, or very painful area of the breast, contact your healthcare provider before booking. These can be signs of mastitis.

Consent and draping

  • Explicit written consent is required before any breast or chest work. It's part of the intake forms, where you choose: no chest work, over the sheet only, or undraped.
  • I'll also confirm out loud before starting, every time.
  • Only the area being worked on is uncovered, and only while it's being worked on.
  • You can change your mind or stop at any point, and I'll stop immediately.
