Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Massage For Motion LLC · Effective November 25, 2026

This policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your health information. It's written to meet Washington's My Health My Data Act. In short, I collect only what I need to give you a safe massage, I never sell or share it, and you can ask me to delete it at any time.

What I collect

  • Contact details: name, phone, email, emergency contact.
  • Visit location: your address, so I can come to you.
  • Health information: health conditions, injuries, surgeries, medications, allergies, pregnancy, postpartum and lactation details.
  • Treatment information: your goals, pressure preferences, areas to avoid, your consent for specific body areas, and my notes from sessions.

Where it comes from

Directly from you, through my intake forms, booking requests, email, or conversation during a session. I don't buy data or collect it from other sources. This website uses no tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies.

How I use it

Only to schedule and travel to your appointments, decide whether massage is safe for you, plan and adjust your treatment, contact you about your appointments, and keep records of your care. I don't use it for marketing or advertising.

Who I share it with

I do not sell your health data, and I do not share it with anyone, except:

  • Google, as a storage provider. Online forms are saved to a private Google account that only I can access. Google stores the data on my behalf and doesn't use it for its own purposes.
  • When the law requires it, for example a valid court order, or to prevent serious, immediate harm to someone's health or safety.

I won't share your information with anyone else, including your healthcare providers, unless you ask me to in writing.

How I protect it

  • Online forms are sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • Records are kept in a private Google account protected by 2-step verification. Nothing about clients is stored on this website.
  • Paper forms are kept in a locked location and shredded when no longer needed.
  • Appointment notification emails don't include health details.

How long I keep it

As long as you're a client and for any period the law requires for treatment records. After that, I delete or shred it.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Find out whether I hold your health data, and get a copy of it.
  • Learn who it has been shared with. Normally that's no one but Google, as described above.
  • Withdraw your consent, and choose paper forms instead.
  • Have your health data deleted.

To make a request, email loki.for.motion@gmail.com. I may ask a question to confirm your identity before acting. I'll respond within 45 days.

Appeals: if I decline your request, you can appeal by replying to my response with "Appeal" in the subject line. I'll answer within 45 days. If you're still unsatisfied, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General.

Changes

If I change how I handle your health data, I'll update this page and ask for your consent again before using your data in a new way.
