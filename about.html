About Loki, LMT

I started Massage For Motion with one goal: helping people keep moving.

After years as a software developer in the city, I knew I needed something new. I chose massage after a licensed massage therapist fixed chronic knee pain that no doctor could diagnose. That pain had kept me from circus, martial arts, and dance for years.

I graduated from the Port Townsend School of Massage and now focus on helping others win back their passion for movement. I perform and teach cyr wheel professionally, so I know how much preventive care and injury recovery matter for staying active. My years at a desk also gave me a special eye for the wrists, necks, and backs that long workdays wear down.

Who I work with

Circus performers and students (cyr wheel, silks, handstands, rope, straps, hoop, pole, and more), dancers, climbers, and hikers, all the way to gamers and software engineers, plus pregnant and lactating clients.

My approach

I provide therapeutic massage only, and my pressure tends to run deep. Sessions can certainly be relaxing, but if you're after very gentle, non-targeted relaxation massage, another therapist will likely be a better fit. I'll listen to what's going on, explain what I'm doing, and check in about pressure and comfort. You're always in charge of your session.

I do not and will not provide sexual services. Any such request ends the session, and I will report it to the authorities.
