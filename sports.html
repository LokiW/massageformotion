Sports Massage

From one performer to another: bodywork that helps you train harder, recover faster, and stay in the game.

I perform and teach cyr wheel professionally. Massage is what got me back to circus, martial arts, and dance after years of chronic knee pain that no doctor could diagnose. That's why I became a massage therapist, and why I focus on keeping people moving.

Who I work with

Circus performers and students (cyr wheel, silks, handstands, rope, straps, hoop, pole, and more), dancers, climbers, hikers, runners, martial artists, and anyone who asks a lot of their body.

What a session can focus on

Maintenance

Regular work during training to manage tension and catch problem areas early.

Recovery

After a show, competition, or big training block, to ease soreness and restore mobility.

Prevention

Addressing tightness and imbalances before they turn into injuries.

Getting back

Supporting your return to training after a strain, alongside your provider or physical therapist.

Common focus areas include shoulders and rotator cuff, forearms and grip, hips and glutes, hamstrings, and calves.

Trades: I'm open to trading with artists on a case-by-case basis, such as circus lessons, art, or veggies. No harm in reaching out.
